How It Works

🔍 Ingredient Matching: Fuzzy matching with RapidFuzz handles variations like tomato ≈ tomatoes, chilli ≈ chili, capsicum ≈ bell pepper.

❤️ Health Scoring: Each recipe scored 0–100 based on protein, fibre, sugar, sodium, and fat per 100g.

⭐ Ranking: Final score = ingredient match (50%) + health score (30%) + protein bonus − calorie penalty.

📝 Explanations: Deterministic, rule-based explanations — no external LLM APIs.

🧠 ML Extension: Optional RandomForest/XGBoost training pipeline included.

About Scoring

Macro Match (0-100): How well the recipe's protein/carb/fat ratio matches your target.

Nutrient Density: Rewards protein & fiber, penalizes sodium & sugar.

Preference Boost: +15-25 points if you've liked an ingredient in the recipe.

Filters: Allergies, dislikes, and diet type (vegetarian/vegan) are strictly enforced.
